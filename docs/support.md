# Pupu Support

Email **minhthang281198@gmail.com** and describe what happened, which

## Common questions

**Where is my data stored?**
On your iPhone only. Pupu makes no network requests and has no account system.
See the [Privacy Policy](./privacy-policy.md).

**My reminders stopped arriving.**
Check Settings → Pupu → Notifications on your iPhone. If notification permission
was revoked there, Pupu turns the reminders switch off and explains why on its
Reminders screen. Turning it back on re-schedules everything.

**A reminder only works for activities with a time.**
Correct — a reminder needs a moment to fire at, so activities parked in a
time-of-day bucket with no clock time do not get one.

**Will my data survive deleting the app?**
No. Deleting Pupu removes its storage with it.
